RMS Titanic

RMS Titanic set sail on her maiden voyage from Southampton, England on April 10, 1912, bound for New York City. Four days later, on April 14, 1912, she struck an iceberg in the North Atlantic Ocean. The ship sank in the early hours of April 15, 1912 resulting in the loss of 1,517 lives.

HMHS Britannic

Designed as a luxury liner by the White Star Line, HMHS Britannic was repurposed as a hospital ship during World War I. After completing five successful medical voyages, she struck a German naval mine on November 21, 1916, in the Kea Channel. She sank in 55 minutes, resulting in the tragic loss of 1,066 lives.

RMS Olympic

RMS Olympic began its 24-year career with a triumphant maiden voyage from Southampton to New York on June 14, 1911. Nicknamed "Old Reliable," it survived a major collision in 1911, served as a WWI troop transport that rammed a U-boat, and returned to luxury before being retired and scrapped in 1935.
