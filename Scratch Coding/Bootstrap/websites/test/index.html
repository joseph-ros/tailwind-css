20 Years Of Racing History

An Asphalt Journey

Asphalt celebrated its 20th anniversary, marking its evolution from a 2004 Nokia N-Gage mobile game into a cross-platform powerhouse with over 1 billion downloads. The series progressed from pixelated street racing to the stunts of Asphalt 8, culminating in today's esport, Asphalt Legends Unite. Gameloft commemorated this milestone with a "Legacy of Speed" update, offering retro cosmetics and a time-shifting track spanning the franchise's history.

Drive Syndicate

Game Update

We introduced the Drive Syndicate event, marking a major evolution in Asphalt 9: Legends with a narrative-driven gameplay experience centered around underground criminal syndicates. The series progressed from standard career races to high-stakes syndicates like Desert Outlaws, culminating in multi-tiered seasonal operations featuring exclusive hypercars like the Bugatti La Voiture Noire. Gameloft packed these events with unique mechanics, offering hazard levels, syndicate credits, and a distinct noir visual aesthetic spanning different chapters of the game's history.
